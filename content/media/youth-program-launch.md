Today marks the launch of our comprehensive Youth Development Program, designed to discover and nurture young talent in our local community. The program will provide free training sessions, equipment, and mentorship opportunities to aspiring young athletes aged 8-16.

Program Highlights:
- Weekly training sessions led by professional coaches
- Monthly workshops with first-team players
- Annual scholarship opportunities
- State-of-the-art training facilities access

"This program represents our commitment to building a sustainable future for the sport in our community," said the Youth Development Director. "We believe every young person deserves the opportunity to pursue their dreams."

Registration for the first intake opens next week, with sessions beginning in November 2025.
