We are thrilled to announce the signing of international star player Alex Thompson on a three-year contract. This landmark signing represents our continued commitment to building a world-class squad.

Thompson, known for exceptional playmaking abilities and leadership qualities, joins us from European giants FC Barcelona. The 24-year-old brings a wealth of experience, having played in multiple international tournaments.

"I'm excited to join this amazing team and contribute to its future success," Thompson said during the signing ceremony. "The project here is incredibly ambitious, and I can't wait to meet the supporters."

The player will wear jersey number 10 and is expected to make their debut in next week's home fixture.
