We're excited to announce a comprehensive upgrade to our main facility, scheduled to begin next month. This $10 million investment will significantly enhance both player facilities and fan experience.

The renovation project includes:
- Expanded seating capacity to accommodate 5,000 more fans
- New high-performance training center
- Enhanced medical facilities
- Improved concession areas
- Sustainable energy implementations
- New family-friendly zones

Construction will be completed in phases to minimize disruption during the season. The project is expected to be fully completed by March 2026.

"These upgrades demonstrate our commitment to providing the best possible experience for our fans and the best facilities for our athletes," said the facility director.
